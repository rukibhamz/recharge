Design System — v1.0

Recharge
Visual identity guide

A calm, credible system for a product that tells people the truth about their capacity — built on one recurring shape: the arc of a vessel refilling.

01

Foundation

Recharge is warm, not clinical. Every choice below optimises for a person who is tired, possibly anxious about their result, and needs the interface to feel like a calm room rather than a diagnostic form.

Signature
The Arc

An incomplete circle — never a full ring — appears throughout the system: the score visualisation, button end-caps, section dividers, and list markers. It represents a vessel that is filling or emptying, never a finished state.

Voice
Plain, never clinical

Copy avoids diagnostic language. Results are framed as information, not verdicts, even at the most severe burnout level.

Pace
Unhurried motion

Transitions run 250–400ms with gentle easing. Nothing snaps or bounces — the interface should never feel urgent.

03

Typography

Three faces, three jobs. Fraunces carries emotional weight at large sizes — headlines and scores. Public Sans handles every interface and body context. IBM Plex Mono marks anything quantified: percentages, question counts, timestamps.

09

Accessibility & usage

Do

Pair every severity badge with text ("Severe burnout"), never color alone. Maintain 4.5:1 contrast for all body text on Linen.

Don't

Never use Signal Red or Ember decoratively — both are reserved exclusively for burnout severity states.

Do

Use the arc motif sparingly — one signature moment per screen. A results screen earns one; a settings screen earns none.

Don't

Never set Fraunces below 20px — the optical serif loses legibility and warmth at small sizes. Drop to Public Sans instead.
